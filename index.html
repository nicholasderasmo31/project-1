Short Summary About My Game:

The premise of my game is you have to help a detective, to help him solve a murder case. I was inspired by a frog detective website a student made in an old class, and there was a timeline connecting all these HTML websites. You can choose who the murderer is, and that will change the story for good or for worse. The characters in my story are Detective Shiny Head, a world-renowned detective who needs your help. There's also Tiffany, and Little Johnny, who were at the scene of the crime, Greg, who was killed by one of them, and the Mayor.
